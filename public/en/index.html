Which sources work?

A direct media file that allows browser access, or a local file you choose. Recognized containers: WAV, MP3, MP4/M4A, WebM and Ogg.

Platform watch pages such as YouTube, logins, DRM, live streams and playlists are not supported. CORS, expiry or redirects may prevent reading a direct link.

WAV and format limits

WAV is uncompressed audio and is often larger than compressed input. Limits: 120 seconds, 2 channels, 48 kHz and 64 MiB of decoded PCM. No MP3 encoding or silently discarded channels.

Original files get basic container and type checks, not full decoding or a security scan. MP4 and WebM do not guarantee a decodable audio track.

Privacy, cancellation and cleanup

No media proxy, account, telemetry or persistent cache. Direct sources can still see your IP and requests; local processing does not mean fully offline.

Browser decoding cannot reliably be aborted. Cancellation waits for it to settle before another job can start. Clear and page exit release this page's work references; they do not delete saved files or guarantee immediate RAM recovery or removal of system caches.
